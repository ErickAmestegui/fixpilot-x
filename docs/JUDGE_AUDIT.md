# Judge audit — delivery risks, 19 October 2026

Prioritized against implementation, design, impact and idea quality. These are actual limitations or evidence gaps, not predictions of scores. V1 observations remain in `EVIDENCE.md`; V2 has a separate verification record.

| Priority | Actual weakness | Why a judge may care | Required next evidence or action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | V1 is public on GitHub, but V2 publication, public video and Devpost submission confirmation are still pending. | Judges must reach the final version and a complete submission. | Validate and publish V2, test access from another session, record/upload the actual demo and retain the submission receipt. See `GITHUB_DELIVERY.md`. |
| 2 | Alexa+ is simulated; the genuine MCP service is loopback-only, without a native device integration. | Integration scope must be understandable and reproducible. | Keep disclosure in the opening and closing narration; show initialization/discovery/calls against the local server. The permitted simulated route is documented in `SOURCES.md`. |
| 3 | V2 has two original educational assemblies, with no arbitrary-manual import or broader procedure library. | Reusability is demonstrated across two schemas, but coverage remains narrow. | Show the verified 6/6 organizer and independent project persistence; do not imply arbitrary furniture or manual support. |
| 4 | No observed real-user assembly study, manufacturer validation or physical strength/load assessment is recorded. | Practical impact and safety remain unproven outside the software. | Describe the product as an educational prototype; seek user/manufacturer validation after submission. Do not turn software tests into physical safety claims. |
| 5 | The interface is Spanish, commands are bounded, browser speech is optional, and V1's production bundle is about 1.1 MB before gzip. | English judges need a readable route; broad language and performance expectations could exceed demonstrated behavior. | Use English narration/captions, readable commands and button fallback. Rehearse the actual final build; do not invent latency, voice-quality or cross-device benchmarks. |

This audit does not establish that another entrant is stronger or weaker. The comparative page review in `COMPETITION_POSITION.md` is explicitly limited to public declarations.
